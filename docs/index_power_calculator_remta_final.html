Meta-Analysis in Statistical Genetics

A hands-on course covering GWAS and ExWAS meta-analysis using METAL and REMETA, with worked examples and exercises modelled on the SISG teaching format.

GWAS ExWAS WGS METAL REMETA REGENIE R
Inspired by: SISG 2025 Module QG3 by Joelle Mbatchou & Michael Epstein (joellembatchou.github.io/SISG2025_Association_Mapping). Exercises here extend that module to multi-cohort meta-analysis.

Session Format

Each practical session follows the SISG format:

  • ~20 min background reading and concept introduction
  • ~40 min hands-on exercises in R (with code scaffolding)
  • ~15 min discussion — compare your results with the answer key
