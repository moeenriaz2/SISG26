GWAS Power Calculator

Adjust the sliders to see how sample size, effect size, allele frequency and case fraction affect statistical power at the genome-wide significance threshold.

Parameters

Formula (Purcell et al., Am J Hum Genet, 2003)

λ = β² × 2p(1−p) × N_eff
N_eff = 4N × π(1−π)
β = ln(OR), p = MAF
Power = Φ(√λ − 5.45)
